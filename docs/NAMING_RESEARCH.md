## A2. Verdict

**Choose PopToPC.** The domain, store and handle situation is identical for both names — the
decision rests entirely on trademark risk:

- `TURNTO` is a **live registered trademark in Class 042** (hosted computer services / SaaS).
  `TurnToPC` adopts that identical leading element in the same commercial area. That is the classic
  fact pattern for a likelihood-of-confusion refusal or an opposition.
- `POPTOPC` and `POPTO` have **zero** marks, and a coined word is far easier to register and defend.

Caveat unchanged: web/X/LinkedIn presence for both names could not be confirmed because every
search engine rate-blocked this machine. Do one manual browser check before paying.

# Appendix B — Google Play publishing costs (paid vs free)

Source: Google Play Console Help, pages 6112435, 13628312, 13634885, 14151465, 9859455.

## B1. Mandatory Google costs

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Play Console registration | **US$25 one-time** | Non-refundable. Credit/debit card only (MasterCard, Visa, Amex, Discover US-only, Visa Electron outside US). **Prepaid cards not accepted.** |
| Per-app fee | **$0** | Unlimited apps on one account |
| Renewal / subscription | **$0** | The $25 is genuinely one-time |
| Content rating (IARC) | **$0** | Questionnaire, built into Play Console |
| App signing / testing tracks | **$0** | Internal + closed testing free |

**Total mandatory Google spend: US$25, once.** Nothing else is payable to Google to host a *free* app.

## B2. Is a domain required?

| Account type | Website/domain required? | What is required instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | ❌ **No** | Government ID + address under your legal name, verified contact email/phone. Privacy policy at *any* active public URL — free hosting (GitHub Pages, Google Sites, Notion) is accepted. |
| **Organization** | ✅ **Yes** — "Organization website" is a mandatory field | Plus **D-U-N-S number** (free from Dun & Bradstreet), organization name, address, phone |

So: **a domain is not required for a personal account**, but it becomes effectively mandatory for an
organization account. Google's wording for the privacy policy is "available on an active URL" — not
"on your own domain".

## B3. Non-money costs people forget

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Closed test: 12 testers × 14 days** | Personal accounts created after 13 Nov 2023 must run a closed test with ≥12 testers opted in **continuously for 14 days** before applying for production access. This is the single biggest schedule cost — plan ~3 weeks. |
| Device verification | New personal accounts must prove access to an Android device via the Play Console mobile app |
| Identity verification | Government ID + address, matching the payment profile name exactly, or the $25 is not refunded |
| Contact email/phone | Must stay operational for the life of the account (OTP-verified) |

## B4. Only if you sell

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Payments profile / merchant account | $0 to open |
| Google Play service fee on sales | 15 % (first $1M/yr) or 30 % |
| Sales tax / GST | Handled by Google for most jurisdictions; a GST registration may be needed in India to sell |

`$25` is charged in USD; your bank adds FX markup, and local tax (e.g. India GST) may be added at
checkout depending on billing country — **verify the final amount on the payment screen**, as this
was not confirmable from the help pages alone.

## B5. Cheapest legal route for this project

1. Personal developer account → $25 once.
2. Privacy policy on **free** GitHub Pages / Google Sites → $0, no domain needed.
3. Optional but recommended: buy `poptopc.com` ($6.48 yr-1 promo, then $11.28) and `poptopc.in` ($9.98/yr) for branding and a real privacy-policy URL.
4. Budget ~3 weeks of calendar time for the 12-tester closed test.

**Realistic first-year total: $25 + ~$16.46 (both domains) ≈ $41.46.** Year 2+: ~$21.26 in domains, no further Google fee.
