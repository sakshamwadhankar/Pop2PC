### Full description (4000 chars max — 2,106 used)

```
PopToPC turns the Android phone you already own into a desktop computer. Connect it to
any Windows PC over Wi-Fi or a USB cable and the PC becomes a full-screen desktop that
runs Windows apps, Linux apps and Android apps — all of them executing on the phone.

HOW IT WORKS
Your phone does the computing. Your PC only shows the picture and sends mouse and
keyboard, like a monitor. Nothing is installed on the PC except a small receiver, and
nothing is left behind when you exit.

- Open Windows programs (Notepad++, 7-Zip and most everyday .exe apps) through the
  built-in compatibility layer.
- Run Linux apps in the built-in X11 desktop.
- Use Android apps you already have, plus apps from Google Play, in desktop windows.
- Build real code with full developer tools such as VS Code.

WHY PopToPC
- One PC for everyone: an old laptop can become a family desktop.
- Privacy by design: your screen, sound, keyboard and files travel only between YOUR
  phone and YOUR PC on YOUR network. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics.
- Pairing is verified with a six-digit code, so only PCs you approve ever connect.
- Your phone stays fully usable during a session — calls and messages keep working.
- While connected the PC is locked to the desktop with a single Exit button; one press
  returns the PC to exactly what it was. No password, no traces.
- Sessions survive interruptions: unplug, move networks or take a call, and the same
  apps with the same content are there when you reconnect.

WHAT TO EXPECT (HONEST LIMITS)
- Not every Windows .exe runs; large or very new programs may fail or run slowly. The
  built-in App Center rates apps so you know before installing.
- Heavy 3D games are not smooth on low-end phones.
- Some Android apps (typically banking apps) refuse to run as copies.

PRIVACY
PopToPC has no servers and no accounts. The desktop stream is a direct TLS-encrypted
connection between your phone and your PC on your local network or your USB cable. We
never see your screen, your keyboard, your files or any other data. Read the full
privacy policy at the link below.
```

## 2. Privacy policy text (host at a public URL — Play requires the URL)

> The owner must publish this text (for example as a public GitHub page or gist) and put
> the URL in Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.

```
PopToPC Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-11-07

What PopToPC does
PopToPC turns an Android phone into a desktop computer that is shown and controlled from
the owner's own PC. The desktop, its apps and all data live on the phone.

What data leaves your device
Nothing is sent to us. PopToPC has no developer-run servers, no analytics, no crash
reporting service, no accounts and no advertising. The app makes no internet connection
except to your own PC.

What travels between YOUR devices
While you use PopToPC, the following travel over a TLS 1.3 encrypted connection directly
between your phone and the PC you have paired and approved, on your local network or
your USB cable:

- the desktop picture (video) generated by your phone,
- your mouse and keyboard input from the PC,
- the desktop's sound (only if you turn sound on),
- files you deliberately transfer between the phone and the PC,
- the clipboard content you copy while the desktop is connected.

This data exists only between your devices. We cannot access it.

Paired-PC list: the phone stores a short fingerprint and the date of each PC you have
approved, so you do not have to re-approve it. You can remove any or all trusted PCs in
the app at any time (Settings → Trusted PCs → Forget).

Permissions and why they are needed
- Internet: the direct connection to your PC.
- Notifications: to show the pairing code prompt and the "desktop running / Stop"
  notification while the PC is connected.
- Microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO): used only with Android's screen-capture consent
  to capture the PopToPC desktop's own sound. Your microphone is never recorded and no
  other app's audio is captured.
- Foreground services: to keep the PC connection alive while you use your phone for
  other things (marked as connected-device/data-sync and, for sound, media projection).
- Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): keeps the desktop responsive during a session when the phone
  would otherwise sleep.
- USB accessory: to detect when you plug the phone into a PC by cable.

Children
PopToPC does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including
children, because no data is collected at all.

Data deletion
There is no account and no server-side data, so there is nothing to delete from our
side. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone; the PC receiver leaves no
data except the trusted-phone fingerprint you can remove from the receiver.

Contact
Contact the developer address published on the Play Store listing.
```

## 3. Data safety form (Play Console → Data safety)

Google's definitions: "collected" = transmitted off the device in a way the developer can
access; "shared" = transferred to a third party. The PopToPC stream is peer-to-peer
between the user's own devices, and the developer has no access to it, so:

| Question | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** | No developer-run server, no analytics, no ads, no accounts. The screen/input/sound/files stream exists only between the user's own phone and the user's own PC over the user's own network; the developer has no access. |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** (answer for the local stream itself) | The phone↔PC stream is TLS 1.3 with pinned pairing (six-digit code verified fingerprints). |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **N/A** (no data collected) | Nothing is stored anywhere the developer controls; uninstall removes everything local. |

### Data types tab
Leave every data type (location, personal info, photos, etc.) unticked — none is
collected, none is shared.

## 4. Permissions declared in the manifest (one-line justifications)

From `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` (the real list):

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | The TLS stream between the phone and the user's own PC (TCP 47600) and mDNS discovery. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | The link service keeps the PC session alive while the phone is used normally. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE` | Declares the foreground service type for the PC connection (cable/Wi-Fi link). |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` | Declares the foreground service type for file/clipboard sync during a session. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION` | Declares the foreground service type used while capturing the desktop's own sound. |
| `RECORD_AUDIO` | Only to satisfy Android's requirement for AudioPlaybackCapture of the PopToPC desktop's own audio after the per-session screen-capture consent. No microphone recording. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | The pairing-code prompt ("Pair this PC?") and the running "Stop" notification. |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Keeps the session responsive while the phone would otherwise doze mid-use. |

`android.hardware.usb.accessory` feature is declared `required="false"` (cable is
optional). There is **no** VPN service, no location, no storage permission, no contacts,
no accessibility service, no system-only permission.

## 5. Console compliance points for the manager to tick

- **Target API level:** `targetSdk 36` (`compileSdk 36`, from `android/app/build.gradle.kts`)
  — meets the 2026 Play target-API requirement; tick "meets target API" in the console.
- **16 KB page sizes:** the app ships native libraries; Play Console flags uploads with
  `.so` files. This build is compliant: **all 15 arm64-v8a ELF libraries have
  `PT_LOAD p_align = 0x4000` (16 KB)**, including `libblackbox.so` and `libdobby.so`
  (verified live in T13-6, re-verified for this release build — see
  `Temp\opencode\t14-1\` evidence). Tick the "16 KB page size compatible" declaration.
- **Foreground service types:** `connectedDevice|dataSync|mediaProjection` are declared on
  the service AND in the permissions list above; the console FSG declaration must name
  these three with the justifications in section 4 (media projection = desktop sound
  capture only, after explicit per-session user consent).
- **Screen capture consent:** the app uses `createScreenCaptureIntent()` once per session
  when the user turns sound on; describe it as "Android's standard screen-recording
  dialog, used only to capture the desktop's own sound".
- **Privacy policy:** paste the section-2 text behind a public URL (owner step).
- **App access:** "All functionality is available without special access" — no login,
  no restricted feature (tick and move on).
- **Content rating questionnaire:** no user-generated content, no accounts, no sharing
  between users, no gambling → expect Everyone.
- **Ads:** contains no ads.
- **Financial features / news / health:** none (do not tick).
- **Device categories:** phone + large screens work; no Android TV/Wear/Car builds.
- **Internal testing track:** upload the `.aab`, add the owner's tester e-mail, roll out
  to internal testing first (this task's release gate).
